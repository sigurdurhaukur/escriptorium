Icelandic OCR

The first Icelandic OCR benchmark, built by volunteers

This is the official annotation platform for the first benchmark for Icelandic OCR. Kickstarted by Sigurdur Haukur Birgisson as part of his research at the University of Groningen, it relies on volunteers to transcribe historical Icelandic documents.

How it works

  • 1

    Scan documents

    We start with scanned historical Icelandic documents — newspapers, manuscripts, and letters.

  • 2

    Run the OCR model

    A pre-trained OCR model produces a first draft transcription of every page.

  • 3

    Correct the errors

    Volunteers manually correct the mistakes in the draft, building a growing corpus of verified transcriptions.

  • 4

    Retrain & repeat

    The corrected pages are used to retrain the model. As more errors are corrected the model improves, and the cycle repeats.

With every corrected page the model gets more accurate.

Getting started

  • 1

    Email me

    Send me a note at sigurdur@sigurdurhaukur.com expressing your interest — no commitment, just curiosity.

  • 2

    Short interview

    We hop on a quick call to understand your research goals and what kind of documents you’d like to help transcribe.

  • 3

    Get access

    I’ll create your account on the Icelandic OCR platform and send you login credentials along with a quick orientation guide.

  • 4

    Annotate data

    Use the browser-based editor to transcribe historical Icelandic pages. Every annotation directly improves the next model training run.

  • 5

    Access the latest models & research

    Contributors get early access to newly trained HTR models, benchmark results, and our active-learning research before public release.
